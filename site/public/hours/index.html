Office hours

These are the public counter hours. If you need something after 4:30, leave a message. We usually call back the next morning.

Office Days Hours
City Clerk Mon-Fri 8:00 a.m. - 4:30 p.m., closed 12:00-1:00
Income Tax Mon-Thu 8:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
Public Works Mon-Fri 7:00 a.m. - 3:30 p.m., garage on Grove Street
Council Chamber 1st and 3rd Tuesday Meeting at 6:30 p.m.

City Hall is closed on federal holidays and the Friday after Thanksgiving. The drop box by the Main Street doors is checked at 8:15 a.m.
